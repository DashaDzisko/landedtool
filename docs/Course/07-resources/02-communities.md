
## Why join a community

Vibecoding is new. Things change fast. New tools appear every week. The best way to keep up is to follow people who are doing the same thing.

Communities also help when you are stuck. Someone probably had the same problem and solved it already.

## Discord servers

- **Cursor** - the official Cursor Discord. People share tips, ask for help, and show what they built.
- **Vercel** - the official Vercel Discord. Good for deployment questions and Next.js help.
- **Supabase** - the official Supabase Discord. Helpful for database and auth questions.
- **Tailwind CSS** - the official Tailwind Discord. Good for styling questions.

## Reddit

- **r/webdev** - general web development. Good for questions and advice.
- **r/nextjs** - specific to Next.js projects.
- **r/ClaudeAI** - people sharing how they use Claude for coding and other tasks.

## How to use communities well

- Search before you ask. Your question was probably answered already.
- When you ask for help, share context. What tool you use, what you tried, and what error you got.
- Share what you build. People appreciate it and give useful feedback.
- Do not compare your progress to others. Everyone learns at their own speed.
